Chimney Inspection in Peoria, IL

NFPA 211 style inspections, a clear report, and a direct quote if work is needed.

Most inspection calls are either a yearly safety check or a house that is being bought or sold. Either way, you should leave knowing what is fine, what needs work, and what it costs if something is wrong. Call (309) 555-0148 or use the form.

When to get an inspection

Yearly checks catch creosote and small water problems before winter. Home sales are different. Buyers and inspectors often want a closer look at the fireplace and flue, not a shrug and a sales pitch.

NFPA 211 is the common industry framework for how deep that look should go. We match the level to the situation instead of upselling a camera job nobody asked for.

Level 1 vs Level 2

  • Level 1: Routine yearly check when nothing major has changed. Readily accessible parts of the chimney and appliance.
  • Level 2: Common after a home sale, weather event, or system change. Covers more of the accessible structure and may include camera scanning when the flue needs a closer look.

What you get

  • Inspection work aligned with NFPA 211 levels (Level 1 or Level 2 as needed)
  • A clear written report of findings
  • Photos of problem areas when present
  • A direct quote if repair or follow-up work is recommended

How the visit goes

  1. Intake - yearly check, home sale, smoke issues, or storm damage.
  2. On-site look - accessible interior and exterior parts for the level you need.
  3. Findings - what is fine, what to watch, what needs fixing, and a price if work is next.
Note: The tech on site will show you what they can see and what they recommend. No mystery invoice later.

Questions

Do I need this before selling?

Often yes. Many buyers and home inspectors flag fireplace and chimney condition. A Level 2 style scope is common around real estate deals.

Is camera scanning always included?

Not always. It depends on access, the inspection level, and whether the inside of the flue needs a closer look.

What if repairs are recommended?

You get options in order of importance. Safety first. Cosmetic or longer-term work is labeled so you can decide.
